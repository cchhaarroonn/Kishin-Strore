✦ FAQ

How do I get my credits?

Enter your exact Minecraft username, pick a pack and pay. The credits are added to your account automatically within about a minute, even if you're offline. Check them in game with /credits.

Can I buy credits for a friend?

Yes. Set their username instead of yours before you pick a pack. The credits go to whoever's name is in the basket.

I paid but didn't get anything.

Wait a few minutes and rejoin. If they still aren't there, open a ticket on our Discord with your username and the transaction ID from your receipt email.

Which payment methods can I use?

Checkout is handled by Tebex, our official payment partner. You'll see every method available in your country (cards, PayPal and more) on the checkout page.

Are purchases refundable?

Credits are a digital good delivered instantly. See the terms on the checkout page, and contact us on Discord if something went wrong.
